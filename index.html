Banshee Band
Band simulator

Listen live or try sample clips

Listening station

Start listening, or choose a sample clip, and see each sound become a ghost, a buzz and a line of text. The microphone is used only after you press Start listening, and the sound stays on this device. No band is connected, so the band is shown on screen.

Works offline after the first visit.

Banshee Band is an aid, not a safety device. It can miss or misread sounds, so keep your smoke alarms and other certified alerting devices.
