Findings:

- White noise (0 and 20 dB, including 85°) and the hum case meet their bounds at the design value 0.01.
- Tones at 10 dB need `reg` near 1 (almost plain cross-correlation) to meet 4°, and the hum case fails from `reg` 0.1 on (62°). The two bounds cannot both hold with the specified weight. Tones at 0 dB fail everywhere (best worst case 17°). PHAT-β 0.5 does not help the tones. Decision: tone rows are graded by the R9.7 rule.
- The clap case (1 Frame of 8) fails at CHUNK 1024 for every `reg`: the 7 noise-only chunks get the same PHAT weight as the clap chunk.

### Clap fix: per-chunk energy weighting (task 3.4, chosen)

Each chunk's PHAT cross-spectrum `W` is multiplied by the chunk energy `sqrt(E_L · E_R)` (sums of squares of the Hann-windowed samples) before it is added to the accumulator. For a stationary signal every chunk has about the same energy, so white noise and hum are unchanged.
